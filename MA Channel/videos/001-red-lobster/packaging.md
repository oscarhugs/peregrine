# 001 Red Lobster: packaging (INTERNAL)

> Internal strategy doc. Built with the internal method in `methods/sexy-canvas/`. **Never name the method, its terms, or its authors in anything published** (titles, thumbnails, descriptions, comments).

## Canvas (diagnosis)
**Persona:** business-curious viewer, 22–50: finance/consulting people, founders, and people who remember eating at Red Lobster. Mostly US.
**Experience:** scrolling the YouTube home feed or search; 0.2 s on the thumbnail, 1–2 s on the title.

| Cell | Concrete input (what exists in the video) | Polarity |
|---|---|---|
| Vanity 👑 | "Everyone blamed the shrimp; you'll know what the documents actually say." The viewer becomes the person who knows the real story. | + |
| Greed | Who got paid: $2.1B price, $1.5B real estate flip, rent collected every year. | + |
| Envy | Starboard saw it in 2014; Wall Street read the fine print. | + |
| Wrath 👑 | At the **myth** and at the fine print. The enemy is the lazy explanation and the structure, **never a named firm and never the viewer**. | inverse |
| Gluttony | Nostalgia (Cheddar Bay Biscuits, Endless Shrimp); binge-able series format. | + |
| Sloth | A complex deal explained in about 15 min, with no finance degree needed. | + |
| Lust | Food imagery (shrimp, lobster). Weak; use as reinforcement only. | + |
| Curiosity 👑 | Open loop: "If not the shrimp, then what?" Resolved in section 06. | + |
| Safety | For owners/operators: the lesson on sale-leasebacks (don't sell the ground you stand on without pricing the next 10 years). | + |
| Belonging | "Deal people" get it; the channel as the club for people who read filings. | + |
| Reward | The receipts: real court filings shown on screen. | + |

**Crown (dominant):** Vanity + Curiosity + Wrath-at-the-myth. Everything else is reinforcement.
**Negatives to watch:** the viewer may already know the "it was PE" take (GEN and More Perfect Union covered it) → our angle has to be *the numbers side by side* and *neutral*, not "PE bad".

## Mechanic line
`MECHANIC: Vanity ("everyone got it wrong") → loop "if not the shrimp, what?" → 15-min doc with the court filings → Curiosity resolved + Safety (the lease lesson) → CTA: subscribe for the next postmortem`

**Objective of the title+thumbnail:** win the click (not the sale). Objective of the first 30 s: win the 10-second "rational reason to stay" (the $11M vs $190M contrast).

## Energies (narration order)
Visionary (cold open: May 2024, the internet's verdict) → Warrior (the $190M number, against the myth) → Master (explains the deal) → Healer (in the verdict: "the fairest verdict the documents support") → Master (lesson) → CTA. The script already follows this; keep it.

## Blacklist (Deal Postmortem: any hit = rewrite)
1. Naming a firm as the killer in a title/thumbnail ("Private Equity KILLED Red Lobster", "Thai Union Destroyed…").
2. "Shocking", "They don't want you to know", "You won't believe", ALL-CAPS rage words.
3. Any number or claim not in `script.md`/`factcheck.md`.
4. Fake urgency or countdowns.
5. AI-generated faces of real people; real people's photos used as "villains".
6. A title that overclaims the verdict. Our verdict: the lease removed the margin and the shrimp was the final mistake. "Died from rent" overclaims; "Wasn't the shrimp" / "the real cost was rent" is fair.
7. Talking down to the viewer ("you're wrong about…"). The wrath points at the myth, not at the viewer.
