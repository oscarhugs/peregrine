# Red Lobster's CEO: From Server to C-Suite

**Early Career**
Damola Adamolekun, 37, began his career as a server at Clyde's restaurant in Maryland during high school. He credits this experience with teaching him essential leadership skills. "You become good at managing difficult people and situations," he explained, noting that servers learn to "lift people from one mindset to another one."

**Path to the Top**
Born in Nigeria to a neurosurgeon and pharmacist, Adamolekun studied at Brown University and worked in investment banking at Goldman Sachs before earning his MBA from Harvard Business School. He later joined billionaire John Paulson's hedge fund and became CEO of P.F. Chang's at age 31.

**Red Lobster Leadership**
Adamolekun took over Red Lobster in 2024 following its bankruptcy, largely caused by the endless shrimp promotion becoming a permanent menu item, which cost the company $11 million in a single quarter. His turnaround strategy includes closing over 100 locations, simplifying the menu, and focusing on guest experience rather than just food service.

**Technology and AI**
He is implementing artificial intelligence to reduce administrative burdens on managers so "they can be out on the floor with the guests," making work more enjoyable.

**Advice for Gen Z**
Adamolekun encourages young workers to embrace AI rather than resist it, treating it "a high-powered assistant or chief of staff" to enhance productivity.
