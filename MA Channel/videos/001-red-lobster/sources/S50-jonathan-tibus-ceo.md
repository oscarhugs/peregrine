# Red Lobster names restructuring expert Jonathan Tibus CEO, replacing Horace Dawson who is retiring

Red Lobster has appointed Jonathan Tibus as its new chief executive officer, succeeding Horace Dawson who is retiring from the position.

Tibus brings extensive experience in corporate restructuring. He has served as managing director at management consulting firm Alvarez Marsal in Atlanta for 22 years. His background includes serving as CEO of Kona Grill when it filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy in 2019, holding the chief restructuring officer position at Krystal when it filed for Chapter 11 in 2020, and serving in the same capacity at Quiznos during its 2014 bankruptcy filing.

The casual-dining chain has faced significant financial challenges, particularly due to an all-you-can-eat shrimp promotion that generated substantial losses. Red Lobster's current majority owner, seafood supplier Thai Union, has been attempting to sell the chain and indicated it will not provide additional financial support.

Dawson had served as executive vice president and general counsel before becoming CEO in September. He replaced Kelli Valade, who stepped down in April 2022 after just eight months in the role. Red Lobster's CEO position had remained vacant for several months prior to Dawson's appointment.
