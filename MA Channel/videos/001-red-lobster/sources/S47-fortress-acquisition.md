# Red Lobster to be Sold to Fortress Investment Group

Fortress Investment Group is acquiring Red Lobster through a credit bid, using debt owed to the firm as currency rather than conducting a traditional sale. The company served as the stalking horse bidder, establishing the initial purchasing baseline. An auction scheduled for Tuesday was canceled after no competing bids emerged.

Court approval for the sale is set for July 29. Fortress is the parent company of SPB Hospitality, which operates Logan's Roadhouse, Old Chicago, Krystal, and other restaurant concepts previously acquired from bankruptcy.

Red Lobster filed Chapter 11 bankruptcy in May after years of financial hardship. The chain closed 93 locations immediately, with plans to reject 108 additional leases. The company cited "a tough economic environment, increased competition, and strategic missteps, including an Ultimate Endless Shrimp promotion" as contributing factors.

Financial challenges included nearly $300 million in debt, with $264.7 million owed to Fortress and $29.2 million to Wells Fargo. Since 2019, Red Lobster experienced a 30 percent decline in annual guest count and a 60 percent drop in EBITDA over the past year, resulting in a $76 million net loss in fiscal 2023. The company now operates 551 U.S. locations and 27 Canadian restaurants.

Red Lobster additionally faces a California lawsuit from employees alleging violations of the WARN Act for failing to provide required 60-day notice before mass layoffs.
