# "A great day for Red Lobster": Seafood chain to exit bankruptcy after approval of restructuring plan

Red Lobster will be acquired by RL Investor Holdings LLC, an entity created by funds managed by affiliates of Fortress Investment Group LLC, alongside co-investors TCW Private Credit and Blue Torch. The acquisition is anticipated to close before the end of September.

Jonathan Tibus will step down as CEO and be replaced by Damola Adamolekun, the former CEO of P.F. Chang's.

Adamolekun stated: "This is a great day for Red Lobster. With our new backers, we have a comprehensive and long-term investment plan."

Red Lobster filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy on May 19 after closing nearly 50 locations. The company was more than $1 billion in debt with less than $30 million in available cash. The restaurant chain's "endless shrimp" promotion resulted in $11 million in operating losses, with investigations revealing a pattern of mismanagement by majority owner Thai Union.

The company currently operates 578 restaurants across 44 states and Canada, serving approximately 64 million customers annually.

Red Lobster has also launched "Cheddar Bay 2024," a promotional campaign offering customers chances to win complimentary Cheddar Bay Biscuits and seafood for four years through November 10, 2024.
