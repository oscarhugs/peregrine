# Red Lobster Shareholders Allege Endless Shrimp Promotion Was Profit-Squeezing Plot

Red Lobster shareholders filed a May lawsuit in Orange County, Florida claiming Thai Union Group, the chain's former majority shareholder, deliberately pushed the $20 "Ultimate Endless Shrimp" promotion to extract maximum value at Red Lobster's expense.

The lawsuit alleges that "Thai Union doubled down on a campaign to squeeze out every drop of value" through deals benefiting the seafood producer while harming the restaurant chain. The endless shrimp promotion lost the company $11 million in a single quarter.

According to the shareholders, Thai Union pressured Red Lobster to make the seasonal promotion permanent in 2023, transforming it into "a car crash." The shift allegedly benefited Thai Union, which became the chain's exclusive shrimp supplier, while devastating Red Lobster's finances through uneconomical pricing.

The promotion contributed to Red Lobster filing for Chapter 11 bankruptcy in May 2024. The company closed approximately 130 locations and emerged from bankruptcy in September 2024 under new CEO Damola Adamolekun, who famously stated he knew "how to do math" regarding the promotion's losses—though the chain brought it back on a limited basis in April 2026.

Shareholders are seeking to dissolve roughly $32 million in transactions and demand damages through a jury trial.
