# Golden Gate Capital sells remaining stake in Red Lobster

Golden Gate Capital has sold its remaining equity stake in Red Lobster to Thai Union Group, the Seafood Alliance investor group, and Red Lobster management. Golden Gate originally purchased the chain from Darden in 2014 for $2.1 billion.

Thai Union, a publicly traded global seafood supplier, previously invested $575 million in Red Lobster in 2016, acquiring a 25% stake plus an additional 24% stake convertible in 2026. Red Lobster will remain headquartered in Orlando, Florida, under CEO Kim Lopdrup's leadership. Financial terms were not disclosed.

## Market Context

Analysts predicted Red Lobster would struggle during the pandemic. Moody's downgraded the chain to a negative outlook in spring 2020, noting that "Red Lobster, with its seafood-centric menu, makes it an easier for groups to veto."

The chain faced significant challenges including a complicated seafood supply chain restart, a $380 million loan due the following summer, and reduced seafood availability as fishermen avoided going out during pandemic peaks.

Despite these pressures, Red Lobster had grown sales to nearly $2.5 billion in 2019, a 1.6% increase year-over-year. By August 31, 2020, 99% of company-owned locations were open for takeout and delivery, with 88% of dining rooms operational.

The company implemented pandemic adaptations including touchless delivery, contactless curbside pickup, and Family Feast offerings, maintaining "off-premise sales at triple our pre-crisis levels."
