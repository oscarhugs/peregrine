# Red Lobster's new CEO said the seafood chain's endless shrimp offering caused an all-you-can-eat chaos

Red Lobster has appointed 35-year-old Damola Adamolekun as its new CEO following the chain's May bankruptcy filing. The seafood restaurant filed for Chapter 11 protection after more than 50 locations closed due to massive losses from its Endless Shrimp promotion.

Adamolekun, formerly chief executive of P.F. Chang's, has identified the unlimited shrimp deal as a major contributing factor to the company's financial troubles. He explained that the promotion, which offered two types of unlimited shrimp dishes for $20 starting in June 2023, proved too expensive to sustain while simultaneously creating operational chaos.

"When you have endless shrimp, and people are coming in and sitting down at the table and eating for hours as much shrimp as they possibly can, you stress out the kitchen," Adamolekun stated regarding the service and staffing challenges the deal created.

The Ultimate Endless Shrimp promotion resulted in an $11 million loss before it was discontinued in late 2023. The restructuring team directly attributed it as a bankruptcy contributor.

Adamolekun's turnaround strategy focuses on operational improvements rather than expansion. He plans to close no additional locations and instead invest in infrastructure upgrades across the chain's 545 remaining restaurants, including HVAC repairs, carpet replacement, and menu streamlining. He emphasized that customer satisfaction depends on "quality food in a comfortable setting."
