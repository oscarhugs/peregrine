# Red Lobster Exits Bankruptcy with New Owner and CEO

Red Lobster completed its exit from Chapter 11 bankruptcy on Monday under new ownership by RL Investor Holdings LLC, emerging as a smaller chain with 545 locations across 44 U.S. states and four Canadian provinces.

## Leadership Changes

Damola Adamolekun, former CEO of P.F. Chang's, now leads the company. He replaces Jonathan Tibus, a restructuring specialist hired in March to guide the company through bankruptcy proceedings.

Adamolekun expressed optimism about the turnaround: "Red Lobster is now a stronger, more resilient company, and today is the start of a new chapter in our history."

## Financial Support

The new ownership structure includes backing from Fortress Investment Group, which also owns SPB Hospitality and restaurant brands including Krystal and Logan's Roadhouse. The parent company has committed to investing more than $60 million in improvements across the organization.

Additional investors include TCW Private Credit and Blue Torch.

## Background

Red Lobster filed for bankruptcy protection in May after facing significant operational and financial challenges. The acquisition by creditors was the proposed resolution during the bankruptcy filing process.
