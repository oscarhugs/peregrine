# Red Lobster Appoints Ex-P.F. Chang's CEO to Lead Bankruptcy Exit

Fortress Investment Group has named Damola Adamolekun as the new CEO of Red Lobster following the chain's emergence from Chapter 11 bankruptcy. Adamolekun previously led P.F. Chang's for approximately three years before departing in August 2023 to rejoin Paulson & Co.

The appointment is contingent upon a Florida bankruptcy judge approving Red Lobster's restructuring plan, with the court scheduled to consider the proposal on September 5. Under the new ownership structure, Red Lobster will be controlled by a lender-backed entity created by Fortress, along with co-investors TCW Private Credit and Blue Torch.

In a statement, Adamolekun expressed his commitment to "reinvigorate the brand by making it the best place to work for our employees and improving the experience for our guests."

Red Lobster filed for bankruptcy protection in May 2024. The seafood chain currently operates more than 540 locations across 44 states. Fortress, a major creditor, has provided $100 million in debtor-in-possession financing to support the company through its Chapter 11 proceedings.
